SAFETY FIRST

Smart Safety Recommendations

Follow these recommendations when traveling through high-risk areas.

🐢

Reduce Speed

Reduce your speed when entering accident-prone areas or poor road conditions.

📱

Avoid Distractions

Do not use your phone while driving and maintain full attention on the road.

🌧️

Drive Carefully in Bad Weather

Rain and fog can reduce visibility and increase stopping distance.

🛣️

Consider Safer Routes

When possible, choose routes with lower predicted accident risk.

ABOUT SAFEROUTE AI

Making Transportation Safer Through Artificial Intelligence

SafeRoute AI is a transportation and mobility platform designed to help people understand road accident risks before they travel.

The system can combine historical accident records, traffic density, weather, road conditions, location, time, and other environmental factors to estimate accident risk.
